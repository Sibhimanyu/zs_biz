Quick questions

Can I arrive late at night?

Yes. Tell us your expected arrival time and we hold the room until three hours after it, guaranteed by a card on your booking. We message you two hours before to check you're still coming.

What can I change after booking?

Your arrival time, the guest name, the room type or the night, subject to availability.

What if the night I want is full?

We tell you straight away, then offer another room, ZO Goa, or the value of your stay as ZO Credits.

Who do I call?

+91 22 4000 1234 or reservations@zohotel.in, day or night.
