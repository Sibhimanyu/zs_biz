Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage my booking?
Go to your confirmation email or Manage Booking to change dates, switch rooms or request a refund. Date changes are free up to 24 hours before arrival.
How do refunds work?
Refunds are processed to your original payment method as per the rate plan's terms, typically within 5-7 business days.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, major debit and credit cards, and net banking. All payments are processed securely.
Can I change my booking?
Yes. Date changes are free and confirm instantly, up to 24 hours before your arrival date, from Manage Booking.
What time is check-in?
Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be requested, subject to availability.
Can I make special requests?
Yes. Add parking, an early check-in, a room view preference, an extra bed, or food preference during booking or by contacting support.
