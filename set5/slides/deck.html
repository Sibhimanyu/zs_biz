The Data · Narrowing down

Who we are writing for: guests who book at short notice.

The question: who leaves in the last 24 hours?

Guests who book within a week of arrival are the ones most likely to drop out.

The Data · How likely

Booking close to arrival is the strongest warning sign.

The question: of the cancelled bookings of each type, how many were lost in the last 24 hours?

Booked within a week: up to 81% of cancellations come in the last 24 hours. Booked three or more months ahead: 2%, because they leave early.

Part A — Why It Works

Four choices that keep it fair.

Pressure-free

Skipping to cancel is always one click away.

Dates are one answer of several

Late hold, a new guest name, a different room or night.

A thank-you, not a payment

₹350 in credits after the stay, for guests who stay.

Holds that cannot sit empty

Time-limited and card-guaranteed, so a room is not blocked all night.

Reaches the 707 guests who press Cancel. The no-shows get reminders instead.
Part A — The Thank-You Credit

Why ₹350 and not more?

Suppose the offer keeps one guest who was about to leave. How many credits can that one saved night pay for?

1 · What we save
₹4,900
one night we would otherwise lose
÷
2 · What each credit costs
₹350
paid to every guest who takes it
=
3 · Credits it pays for
14
guests who get the credit

one guest who was about to leave, saved. The other 13 would have stayed anyway, and still get the credit.

So ₹350 pays for itself if one in every 14 guests who get it was about to leave. A ₹700 credit would need one in every seven, which is harder.

Assumes a 30% chance of reselling the room, so about ₹4,900 of a ₹6,999 night is at risk. The credit is paid after check-out and valid for 90 days. We would test it on half of guests first.

Style guides

How we followed our two guides.

Confirmation messages guide
Same verb in the header, description and button
“Cancel your Family Room reservation?” · “If you cancel…” · “Cancel reservation”
No “Are you sure” and no exclamation points
The header states the action and nothing else
Specific buttons, confirm on the right
“Keep reservation” on the left, “Cancel reservation” on the right
Short, relevant description only
One answer card for the reason picked
Zoho Style Guide
Numbers: spell out zero to nine, numerals from 10
“three hours”, “24 hours”, “one night”, “7%”
Dates and times: month, day, year; “Oct.”; “2 PM”
“Monday, Oct. 5, 2026” · “2 PM check-in”
Active voice, no filler words
“We charge one night”, “We’ll hold the room”; no “just” or “really”
Money: symbol first; comma in thousands
“₹6,999”; ranges with an en dash
